Conference Information

For Presenters

Presentation information

  • Talk format: Talks will be 10 minutes with 3 minutes for questions. Session chairs will enforce this timing to ensure all presenters have equal time.
  • Slides: Please upload your slides here by 11:59pm on October 9th into the folder for your session. Use the naming convention explained in your registration confirmation email.
  • Poster presenters: If you have indicated that you’d like us to print your poster for you, it must be emailed to amattso1@jhu.edu by 11:59pm on October 6th. Maximum poster dimensions are 42in x 48in and they should be sent to me as a PDF (or PNG, JPEG, TIFF) in the proper final dimensions.
For Attendees

Getting to the conference

  • Getting there: The Bloomberg Center for Physics and Astronomy is located at 3701 San Martin Drive, Baltimore, MD 21218. The conference will be held in Room 478.
  • Entrance: The south east doors of the Bloomberg Center will be open for entry. There will be a check-in table near this entrance.
  • Parking: For those that indicated they would need parking, please park in the San Martin Garage (located at 3500 San Martin Drive, Baltimore, MD 21218). When you arrive, please take a parking ticket and then walk over to the Bloomberg Center. The pass provided will be used as validation when you exit the garage.
Please email amattso1@jhu.edu with any questions or concerns.
